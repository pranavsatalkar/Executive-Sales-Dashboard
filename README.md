# Executive Sales Dashboard | Power BI

## Project Overview

The **Executive Sales Dashboard** is an interactive Business Intelligence project developed using Microsoft Power BI to analyze sales performance, revenue, profitability, products, customers, and sales channels.

The dashboard provides an executive-level overview of business performance through interactive visualizations and key performance indicators (KPIs). It helps stakeholders identify sales trends, evaluate product performance, and make data-driven business decisions.

## Dashboard Preview

### Page 1: Executive Overview

![Executive Sales Dashboard - Overview](images/Overview.png)

The overview page provides a consolidated view of sales performance, including revenue by country, revenue and profit trends, and regional performance.

![Executive Sales Dashboard - Product and Customer Analysis](images/Analysis.png)

The analysis page focuses on product performance, category contribution, customer types, and sales channel distribution.

## Business Objectives

- Monitor overall sales and profitability.
- Analyze revenue performance across countries and regions.
- Identify the top-performing products by revenue.
- Understand revenue contribution across product categories.
- Compare customer types based on revenue.
- Evaluate the performance of different sales channels.
- Support executive decision-making through interactive reporting.

## Dashboard Features

### 1. Executive Overview
- Revenue analysis by country.
- Revenue and gross profit trends over time.
- Regional revenue and gross margin comparison.
- Executive-level KPI cards.

### 2. Product & Customer Analysis
- Top 5 products by revenue.
- Product category revenue contribution.
- Revenue comparison by customer type.
- Sales channel revenue distribution.

### 3. Interactive Filtering
The dashboard includes interactive slicers for:
- Month
- Quarter
- Region
- Country

These filters allow users to explore sales performance across different business dimensions.

## Key Business Insights

Based on the dashboard's displayed values:

- **Country Performance:** The United Kingdom is the leading country by revenue, followed by Germany and France.
- **Regional Performance:** Western Europe has the highest displayed revenue among the four regions, at approximately 198K.
- **Product Performance:** Summit Backpack 45L is the highest-revenue product among the displayed top five products, generating approximately 65K.
- **Category Contribution:** Camping contributes approximately 30.77% of total revenue, followed by Hiking at 27.89%.
- **Customer Analysis:** Returning customers generate the highest displayed revenue, at approximately 226K.
- **Sales Channel Analysis:** Online Store is the largest sales channel by revenue contribution, accounting for approximately 40.15%.
